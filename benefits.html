Benefits of Work Site History

  • Reduces time spent searching historical work orders.
  • Improves technician decision making before excavation.
  • Supports better first-time fix outcomes.
  • Retains organisational knowledge when staff move roles.
  • Provides a consistent site briefing for all crews.

Example Query

Question: What work has previously been completed at 15 Kowhai Road?

Expected Response: Summarise previous leak investigations, repairs and maintenance completed at the address and provide any relevant technician notes.
